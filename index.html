Status definitions
  • Completed – task fully executed, approved, or officially established.
  • In Progress – currently active (drawings, fabrication, quotation review, or civil execution).
  • Scheduled – confirmed upcoming milestone or stakeholder meeting scheduled.
  • Under Review – awaiting management review, feasibility sign-off, or budget approval.
  • Pending – awaiting prerequisites, shutdown schedule, or formal Purchase Order issuance.
